How we engage

Project, managed service, extended team or advisory

  • Fixed-scope project

    Assessment, implementation, migration or build with a defined scope, plan and acceptance criteria.

  • Managed service

    Application management under SLAs: monitoring, support, patching and enhancements with flexible capacity.

  • Extended team

    Named consultants and developers embedded with your team for a programme or a year, under your governance.

  • Advisory

    Short, senior engagements: migration path selection, landscape assessment, planning business case, serialization readiness.
