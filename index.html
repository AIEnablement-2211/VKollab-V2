Who we are

An SAP partner that stays after go-live

VKollab Technologies was founded in 2020 in Parsippany, New Jersey, around a simple model: smaller, more senior teams; the same people from scoping to support; and long client relationships built by working as an extension of the client's own team.

Today more than ninety consultants and developers work from Hyderabad, Guntur and Vijayawada for clients in the United States, Europe and Japan. The work spans the SAP core (S/4HANA, development, Fiori, migration and application management), supply-chain planning (IBP and PP/DS), serialization and compliance for regulated manufacturers (Advanced Track & Trace, ComplianceWire), HR platforms (SuccessFactors, Workday) and, increasingly, AI inside SAP processes.

What is different about working with us

Four commitments that shape every engagement.

Ask us about your programme
  1. 01

    Regulated-industry depth

    Two of our practices exist for pharmaceutical and life-sciences clients: SAP Advanced Track & Trace for serialization and DSCSA/EU reporting, and ComplianceWire for GxP training under 21 CFR Part 11. Few SAP partners of our size offer either.

  2. 02

    One team from assessment to run

    The consultants who scope a programme stay through build, cut-over and hypercare, then run it under an SLA. Knowledge does not leave with the implementer.

  3. 03

    Named consultants, US and India coverage

    Clients know who is on their account. Delivery centres in Hyderabad, Guntur and Vijayawada work alongside the New Jersey office, so support covers US business hours and overnight windows.

  4. 04

    Clean-core discipline

    Custom development follows SAP's clean-core guidance and is documented and transported properly, so the next upgrade stays a standard upgrade.

How we work

The same four stages on every programme

  1. 01

    Assess

    Landscape, custom code, data and the business objective reviewed before any plan is committed. Output: a sized scope, risks and a cost range.
