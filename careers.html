Careers

Work on SAP programmes that go live

VKollab hires experienced SAP consultants and developers in Hyderabad, Guntur and Vijayawada, and occasionally in New Jersey. We are an equal-opportunity employer.

Send your CV
Why VKollab

Senior work, a team that knows your name

With around ninety people we are large enough to run full S/4HANA programmes and small enough that leadership knows every consultant. You work directly with clients, not through layers of account management.

  • Client-facing work from the first assignment, with a senior consultant alongside you
  • Structured training on current SAP releases; technology moves quickly and we keep people current
  • Offices in Ameerpet (Hyderabad), Guntur and Vijayawada; hybrid working by agreement with your practice lead
  • Programmes for clients in the United States, Europe and Japan, with travel where the work needs it
What you will work on

Three areas, all of them live client work

  • S/4HANA and the SAP core

    Conversions, new implementations, custom development, Fiori and application management for US and European clients.

  • Planning and serialization

    SAP IBP, embedded PP/DS, Advanced Track & Trace and ComplianceWire, mostly for pharmaceutical and manufacturing clients.

  • HR platforms and AI

    SuccessFactors and Workday implementations, and AI use cases inside SAP finance, procurement and supply-chain processes.
